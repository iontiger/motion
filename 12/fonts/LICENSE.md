- `blackhansans-sub.woff2`: Black Han Sans. Copyright 2015 Zess Type.
- `bagel-sub.woff2`: Bagel Fat One. Copyright 2022 The Bagel Fat One Project Authors.
- `hahmlet-sub.woff2`: Hahmlet (variable, wght 100–900). Copyright 2021 The Hahmlet Project Authors.
